Shopify Editions

Welcome to Shopify Editions, where twice a year we show you everything that we’ve been building. We know that brands need new ways to engage with their customers—and that means creating new connections. So this release of Editions features everything you need to win in the new era of commerce: Connect to Consumer.

Find new customers by putting your products in front of the right buyers and drive more sales with Shop Cash. Convert every customer across international markets with built-in B2B and Shopify POS. Create powerful and expressive custom storefronts or exclusive NFT commerce experiences. Build long-term relationships with new engagement tools and deeper access to customer insights. Run your business better with a faster, more-streamlined Admin, and easily fulfill every order with SFN and 2-day delivery. If there’s something else you need, apps built on the world’s best commerce platform have you covered.

The C2C era is here. Let's connect.
